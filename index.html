Mika Meitz

Professor of Economics · University of Helsinki

About Me

I am Professor of Economics and Head of the Discipline of Economics at the University of Helsinki. I received my PhD in Econometrics from the Stockholm School of Economics in 2006. From 2006 to 2008, I was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in Economics at University of Oxford. From 2008 to 2013, I was an Assistant Professor in the Department of Economics at Koç University in Istanbul. I have worked at University of Helsinki since 2013 and was promoted to full professor in 2019.

My field is econometrics and my current research interests include machine learning methods, time series, and statistical inference.
